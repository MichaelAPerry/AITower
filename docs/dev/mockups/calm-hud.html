AItower: Calm HUD

Proposed redesign: the building is the UI. The world gets about 85% of the screen. The HUD keeps only what a clicker needs at a glance (money, money per hour, the next goal, speed, inbox). Everything else waits in drawers on hotkeys. Events show in the world instead of in text.

Click around the mockup: the speed buttons, the drawers on the right edge (C, R, F, I), the memo stamps and the build dock. Cash floats up from racks as jobs pay.

Show, don't tell

  • Cash floats up from the rack that earned it. Penalties float red.
  • Broken racks spark and get a pin. The robot walking over is the "repair queued" message.
  • "Per hour" is the clicker number: the one figure that should always go up.
  • Anything affordable glows: research, build items, upgrades.
  • Every number has a colour state (green ok, amber watch, red act), so you rarely need to read it.

Paths without new prose

  • Each Ascension path reskins the building: façade, windows, sky and roof (Biotech flesh and wet glass, Greentech algae and solar, Synthetic glass and cold light).
  • The machines change silhouette, not just colour.
  • The path's music layer and ambient motion carry the mood. Memos stay short.
