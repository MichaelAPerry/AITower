# The bar for dialogue (owner-facing sample)

The first rewrite read as written by a machine: every line a polished quip, people explaining their feelings in full sentences ("I was testing you, and I hate that it worked"), stage-play formality. Real people don't talk like that. This is the bar.

## Bea, "Certified Green" (before → after)

**Before**
> BEA: Okay, good news, big news, I'm so happy. A company will sell us a certificate that says the whole tower is carbon neutral. Plaque for the lobby. Logo for the website. Two hundred grand.
> BEA (after you buy it): It's paper. It's a receipt for a tree somebody promised not to cut down. I was testing you, and I hate that it worked.

**After**
> BEA: Hey. Got a sec?
> BEA: Okay so you know those carbon-neutral certificates? Some company'll do one for the whole building. We'd get a plaque. For the lobby.
> BEA: It's two hundred grand.
> BEA: Or, I mean. There's the north wall. I could put panels up there. They'd be in shade half the day, honestly. They'd run the lifts and that's about it.
> BEA: Anyway. Everybody does the certificate.

*You buy the certificate:*
> BEA: Yeah. Okay.
> BEA: No, it's fine. It's what I'd pick.
> LUCI: The Authority will love it. They love a plaque.
> *(Bea doesn't answer.)*

*You buy the panels:*
> BEA: Wait. Really?
> BEA: Nobody's even going to see them.
> BEA: ...Okay. I'm gonna go get the ladder.

## Rules this shows
1. **Short lines.** Most lines under 12 words. One thought per line.
2. **Contractions and spoken grammar.** "Some company'll", "gonna", fragments. Not every sentence complete.
3. **Subtext, not explanation.** Bea never says she's hurt or that it was a test. The player feels it from "Yeah. Okay." and her silence.
4. **No zingers.** At most one sharp line per scene, and usually from Luci. No aphorisms, no metaphors that sound like a fortune cookie ("a receipt for a tree").
5. **Mundane detail.** Ladders, mugs, the lift, lunch. Not grand imagery.
6. **People react to the last thing said**, sometimes sideways or not at all. They don't make speeches.
7. **Let silence and action carry it.** "(Bea doesn't answer.)" beats a line of dialogue.
8. **Read it aloud.** If a person wouldn't say it out loud to a coworker, cut it.
